Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. ByCan HVAC Engineering is committed to safeguarding personal information in accordance with Ontario and Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA).

1. Information We Collect

When you request technical service, request an estimate, or communicate with ByCan HVAC Engineering via our website, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact details (e.g., full name, phone number, email address).
  • Property & service address within the Greater Toronto Area (e.g., Etobicoke, Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton).
  • Mechanical equipment details, system brand, error codes, and symptoms provided during technical intake.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal and mechanical diagnostic data exclusively to:

  • Dispatch certified gas and refrigeration technicians to your location.
  • Prepare accurate, itemized engineering proposals and estimates.
  • Facilitate manufacturer warranty registrations and Enbridge/Ontario rebate filings on your behalf.
  • Communicate service appointment confirmations and diagnostic findings.

3. Information Protection & Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized ByCan mechanical staff and technical dispatchers. Information is shared externally only when required for TSSA regulatory filings, municipal permit compliance, or authorized rebate administrative processing.
