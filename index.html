THE BYCAN DIFFERENCE

Zero Shortcuts in Mechanical Safety.

Most HVAC contractors rush jobs with cheap slip fittings and flame guesswork. At ByCan, we apply strict European hydronic discipline and precision instrumentation to every boiler, furnace, and heat pump installation.

1. Nitrogen-Purged Copper Brazing Zero Carbon Slag

We flow dry nitrogen through refrigeration lines during oxy-acetylene brazing, preventing internal oxidation that burns out compressor expansion valves.

2. Digital Combustion & Flue Gas Analysis CO Safety Certified

We test CO, O2, and flue temperatures with electronic analyzers on every furnace service to ensure safe venting and zero carbon monoxide leakage.

3. Multi-Zone Hydronic Balancing Equal Thermal Flow

Custom brass manifolds with individual loop flow meters, ensuring radiant floors and cast-iron radiators heat evenly without cold spots.

4. White-Glove Job Site Protection Pristine Cleanliness

Protective floor runners, shoe covers, secure cable conduits, and complete vacuuming. We leave mechanical rooms cleaner than we found them.
